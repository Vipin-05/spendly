Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

At Spendly, we believe your personal finances are your business and nobody else's. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your personal and financial data.

1. Data We Collect

We only collect data necessary to provide you with a reliable and intuitive expense tracking service:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and an encrypted hash of your password.
  • Financial & Expense Data: Any transactions you log, including amounts, categories, dates, payment notes, and descriptions.
  • Usage & Device Information: Standard technical information such as browser type, operating system, and IP address for diagnostic and security purposes.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data collected strictly for delivering and improving Spendly:

  • To authenticate your identity and safeguard your account against unauthorized access.
  • To record, categorize, and calculate your expense summaries and financial visualizations.
  • To provide customer support and notify you of critical system updates.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or share your financial data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

The security of your personal data is a top priority:

  • All sensitive user credentials, such as passwords, are hashed and salted using robust cryptographic algorithms.
  • All data in transit between your browser and our servers is secured using modern TLS encryption.
  • We retain your transaction history and account records for as long as your account remains active. You may request account deletion or data removal at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly works with select third-party infrastructure and hosting providers who adhere to strict data privacy and security standards. These service providers only access data as required to perform their operational roles (such as server hosting and database persistence) and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You retain complete control over your data on Spendly:

  • You can view, edit, or delete any of your logged expenses at any time.
  • You can update your personal account details whenever needed.
  • You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated transaction records.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements to our application or changes in legal requirements. Whenever changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact our privacy team at privacy@spendly.app.
